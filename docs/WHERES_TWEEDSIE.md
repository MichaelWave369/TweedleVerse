# WHERE'S TWEEDSIE? | Stemside Hidden-Scene Comedy

**Format:** Proposed original hidden-object / visual-search adventure  
**Universe:** TweedleVerse / Stemside  
**Status:** Creator-established game concept with proposed features; **not currently playable**  
**Creator:** Michael Wave / Backyard Production Company  
**Related:** [Tweedle 420](TWEEDLE_420.md) · [World Bible](WORLD_BIBLE.md) · [Winnie Kodak III](WINNIE_KODAK_III.md) · [Merv / The Last Exposure](MERV_THE_LAST_EXPOSURE.md)

> Tweedsie has been watching you look for him the whole time.

## Creator-established concept [LOCKED]

- A **Where's Tweedsie?** game: dense, detailed original Stemside scenes where the player searches for Tweedsie and other absurd little background events.
- The comedy comes from the established running gag that **Tweedsie is always wandering through the background**, casually smoking his big fictional spliff, often looking straight at the viewer.
- Genre inspiration: classic crowded-picture puzzles and irreverent adventure-game scavenger hunts. **Do not borrow copyrighted artwork, recognizable compositions, licensed characters, exact jokes, silhouettes, or UI designs.**
- A stand-alone game should also connect thematically to **Tweedle 420's hidden background cameos** and wider TweedleVerse locations.

## Proposed player loop

**Choose an illustrated Stemside location → pan and zoom → observe absurd interactions → identify Tweedsie and optional side details → collect a local sighting → unlock another scene or harmless extra → revisit for hidden jokes.**

- The **primary find** is Tweedsie. He must be present, logically discoverable, and never permanently removed before the player can click.
- Each scene includes **optional comedic finds** with meaningful captions or interactions, rather than a faceless checklist of arbitrarily tiny pixels.
- Optional hidden dialogue, animated reveals, side scenes and nonessential fake-outs reward curiosity. No clue may require a real-world purchase or account.
- Some scenes can use a **small number of animated background routes** (including Tweedsie walking by in opposite directions); the player always has a pause/reduced-motion way to find him.
- A completed find should have a satisfying, original character payoff, not repeated frustrating false-success gags.

## Special mechanic proposals

### 1. Tweedsie sees you

On discovery, Tweedsie may wink or address the player. As an optional gag, he can briefly step outside the illustrated scene or appear near a mouse pointer. **Do not move the required target after the player has fairly found him**, and provide equivalent reactions for keyboard and touch users.

### 2. Winnie portrait challenge

A non-timed side objective asks the player to spot an absurd event in a crowd before selecting an optional Winnie photograph. Winnie sells an unnecessary print package; a print may reveal a bonus clue. **No required camera flashes**, strobing or split-second reactions.

### 3. Dimensional photo scene

Only **special, scarce** photos can act as windows/portals connected to Merv's original camera-ship concept. Ordinary negatives are not unlimited transport keys. A self-contained nested scene can be an optional visual level; no changes to Merv's mission outcome or missing film supply are implied.

### 4. Shared spotting album

A proposed **local-first Spotter's Album** tracks scenes found, optional objects, and playful completion titles. Cross-game achievements may be *read-only* references in the future, but the album must **not alter** existing Passport stamps, game saves, or Crossroads results. No account, cloud leaderboard, tracking pixel or paid unlock required.

### 5. Optional episode replays

If *Tweedle 420* episodes exist later, a paused frame or curated scene can become a separate spotting challenge. That is a **future integration** rather than a claim of working embedded video.

## Distinctive visual identity

An original, hand-authored **dense Stemside panorama**: southside retro-tech storefronts, silicon citizens, contradictory signs, gopher holes, preposterous municipal paperwork, and layered bits of visual comedy. Character and scene art are owned or properly licensed; do not reconstruct *Where's Waldo?* art direction or *Leisure Suit Larry* gag artwork.

- Visual density should support fun, not unreadable pixel hunting.
- Primary silhouette should be recognizable at modest zoom, with difficulty from scene context rather than near-invisible scale.
- Tweedsie's established green/brown silhouette, dreadlocks, sunglasses, pendant, and fictional oversized spliff remain consistent with the [character bible](CHARACTERS.md).
- Large screens: smooth pan and zoom. Mobile: explicit drag/zoom controls and non-overlapping UI. Never require hover.

## Accessibility and fairness

- Each scene has a **structured hint ladder**: scene-region clue → descriptive clue → optional clearly marked reveal. Hints never require watching ads.
- Support zoom, high-contrast outlines, reduced motion, non-flashing cue alternatives, text descriptions, keyboard focus, touch targets, and color-independent verification.
- Provide screen-reader friendly **text exploration mode** or equivalent list of hotspots with meaningful descriptions. Avoid requiring visual acuity as the sole route to story progress.
- Allow pause/retry with no penalty; discoverable targets cannot be lost or randomized into impossible states.
- Make all reveals deterministic/testable, while optional incidental gags may vary.
- For younger audiences, consider age-appropriate presentation of fictional adult smoking references; no real-world consumption directions.

## First playable rung (proposal only)

1. Create **one original TVPD panorama** with Tweedsie and 5–8 optional visual gags.
2. Implement pan/zoom and safe point selection, accessible keyboard + touch hotspot list, hints, completion and reset.
3. Add one optional Winnie photograph reveal; no portals or complex animation yet.
4. Use a **new isolated, versioned local save** if persistence is needed, and provide confirmed reset.
5. Add visual QA and deterministic tests for hotspot positions, hint progression, keyboard and mobile play, no skipped finds, missing assets, and 100% offline load.
6. Expand to South Stemside, Grand Stem and darkroom only after this first scene is enjoyable.
